# Timeline

From the deep past to the final frame of Season 1. Intervals the show states are given
as stated; everything else is marked **(inference)** or **(unstated)**.

---

## Deep past — the making of the gods

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Unstated | Zeus, Hera, Poseidon and Hades are born mortal to Kronos, who torments them for years. Zeus's own phrasing later: *that fuckin' psychopath.* |
| Unstated | Zeus kills Kronos and absorbs his soul at the moment of death. The power is intoxicating. |
| Years after | Zeus works out *how* he did it, and repeats it. And again. (E6) |
| Unstated | Zeus makes Hera queen; the marriage oath is sworn. Hades is given death, Poseidon the sea — assignments Hades resents to this day. |
| Unstated | Zeus tires of killing personally, and orders Hades to build the Frame. Soul harvesting is industrialized and sanitized. Renewal is invented as its cover story. |
| Unstated | The myths are written — deliberately, by Zeus and Hera together, "the way we saw fit" (Hera, E5). This includes the fabricated abduction of Persephone. |

**Childhood-era details.** Prometheus and Zeus knew each other as children: the burned
ants, the week of weeping, and Hestia the puppy who was run over by Kronos's cart and
whom Zeus loved back to life. Prometheus deploys all three in Episode 2 as evidence of
who Zeus used to be.

## Prometheus's fall

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Unstated, thousands of years ago | Prometheus is free, and in love with Charon. |
| Immediately before his imprisonment | An unidentified visitor comes to his door and tells him: it is not his fate to kill Zeus; he must kill Charon; he must regain Zeus's trust and submit to his punishment; there is a greater plan. **The visitor is never identified.** (E5) |
| Same day | Prometheus makes Charon swear to protect a stranger recognizable by a mark, then strangles him. Charon becomes ferryman of the Styx. (E4) |
| After | Prometheus tells Zeus he has become everything he hated. Zeus chains him to the rock; the eagle begins. The public myth blames the gift of fire. (E5) |
| "Thousands of years" | Prometheus waits for the right people in the right place at the right time. (E1) |
| Meanwhile | The relationship with Zeus is slowly rebuilt until Prometheus is his only friend. |

## The fall of Troy

Unstated date; a generation or so before the present **(inference — Nax is a young adult
and was a small child at the fall)**.

- Troy is destroyed. Hecuba's and Andromache's husbands are butchered in front of them.
- Astyanax, Andromache's only child, is thrown from the city walls when the Trojans
  surrender. He survives, and is presumed dead for years.
- Cassandra warns about the horse and the men hidden inside it. Nobody believes her.
- Survivors are given refuge in Krete under segregation: no equal rights, no proper
  schools, no integration. From more than five million Trojans, roughly fifteen thousand
  remain (Nax, E3).
- Prometheus's account of the Trojan War as a template: Zeus seeds enmity, one man steals
  another's wife, a child is burned for a favorable wind, and it runs ten years.

## Krete, roughly thirty years ago

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| ~30+ years ago | Poseidon installs Minos as president of Krete after the previous incumbent proves headstrong and ends up dead. Three rules: it's all on loan, I put you here, and please me and I'll make you immortal. First task: let the lion out of the Heraklion zoo, because it will eat people and be fun. |
| Same meeting | Minos confesses his prophecy forbids children. Poseidon's fix: have twins and kill the firstborn. |
| ~30 years ago | Ari and Glaucus are born. Glaucus first, quiet and blue; Ari second, screaming. |
| Days or weeks later | Minos orders Daedalus — already his prisoner — to kill Glaucus, threatening Icarus. Daedalus takes the baby but does not kill him. |
| After | Glaucus is kept in the dark below the palace, unnamed, unspoken to. He bites, then maims, then kills a jailer. Minos is impressed and has him weaponized. Daedalus builds the mask, the horns, and a bell so the boy will know him. |
| After | Icarus learns what his father did, builds wings to escape, and dies. |
| Ever since | Ari's mother is told the body cannot be viewed and buries a closed coffin. An annual waxwork is commissioned. Ari is told she smothered her twin in her sleep. |

## Riddy's childhood

- Riddy is roughly in her late twenties or early thirties **(unstated)**.
- Her mother becomes a tacita when Riddy is five — cutting out her tongue for Hera.
- Riddy prays to Hera for years to send her mother back. It takes her about ten years to
  accept that her mother is never coming home.

## Season 1 — the present

The season spans roughly **six days**, enumerated below **(inference: dialogue tracks
"last night," "today," and "tonight" continuously, with no stated gaps, so the day
boundaries are reconstructed rather than stated)**.

### Day 1 — Olympia Day
Zeus finds the wrinkle and the Meander blip. The monument is desecrated by the Trojan 7.
Dionysus gives and Zeus rejects the watch. Cassandra warns Riddy. Riddy confesses to her
mother, defies the gods, and is killed. Ari's birthday: the waxwork, the ceremony, the
Munis, the pardon of the Trojans, and their execution at midnight. Poseidon waterboards
Minos. Orpheus performs, then buys a gun. The Furies come for Ari.

### Day 2
Riddy's funeral has taken place **(inference — the coin is placed before the ferry)**.
Orpheus is stopped by Dionysus on the beach. Riddy crosses the Styx and learns she has no
coin. Caeneus is promoted to diver. Orpheus loses the quiz and Dionysus buys his entry
with the watch. Zeus works through his children's answering machines. Hera kills a lover
and a newborn.

### Day 3
Zeus shoots ball boys over the watch. Orpheus and Anatole enter the underworld; Orpheus
kills Anatole on the Styx and is saved from Charybdis. Riddy and Caeneus find Nax and the
Nothing. Dionysus returns the watch. Zeus lays out phase two. The Furies show Ari the
film; she confronts Daedalus and learns Glaucus lives. Zeus executes phase one on Earth.
Zeus listens to the tacitas' tongues and confronts Hera. Hades extracts Nax in front of
Riddy and Caeneus.

### Day 4
Caeneus and Riddy compare prophecies and deduce the truth. Prometheus plants the Minos
idea. Hades confronts Zeus and is struck by lightning. Poseidon consults the Fates. Hera
threatens Minos. Orpheus passes the wilderness and reaches Asphodel. Dionysus discovers
Hera and Poseidon.

### Day 5
Orpheus meets Riddy and is refused by Hades; Persephone releases them both anyway.
Daedalus tells Ari everything and takes her into the labyrinth. Minos kills Glaucus on
live broadcast; Ari kills Minos. Zeus collapses.

### Day 6 — the curtain
Zeus's vision of bleeding. He burns the Fates. Riddy and Orpheus reach the surface and
part. Caeneus's mother arrives and enters the Lethe; he follows and renews her. Zeus
rations the Meander, kills Dennis, burns the bees, seizes every city, and confines Hera.
Prometheus is freed. Cassandra names Riddy a prophet. Hera calls for troops. Dionysus
joins the Trojans.

## Notes on elastic time

- **The underworld's clock** runs alongside Earth's — Caeneus has been dead ten years and
  the Amazons' timeline matches Earth's — but nothing establishes a conversion rate, and
  the two-hundred-year sentence is stated as literal.
- **The six days are six days below, and the show never says what they are above.** Riddy
  dies on Day 1 and spends Days 2–6 in the underworld; Orpheus follows on Day 3. Nothing
  on screen fixes how much Earth time passes while they are down there, and the novelization
  takes the gap — Riddy's death is four months before the curtain, Earth-side. That is
  invention, not evidence: see
  [DC23 in 11-S2-CANON.md](11-S2-CANON.md).
- **Divine ages** are never given. Zeus's mortal childhood is treated as a real memory by
  both him and Prometheus.
- **Hades's neglect of the Nothing** — "I've not been in there for so long" — implies
  decades or centuries of accumulation **(inference)**.
