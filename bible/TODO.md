## 1. Factual errors — transcript contradicts bible

### 1.1 The extraction-pain scene is reversed (E05) — three files ★ largest error
The bible says Caeneus physically felt Hades's extraction of Nax and Riddy felt nothing.
The transcript says the opposite: **Riddy felt it; Caeneus felt nothing.**

Evidence (E05, extraction scene): `[Riddy groans]` during the high-pitched ringing;
"Riddy? Riddy, can you hear me?"; Riddy retches outside and says "I'm fine, I'm fine";
then, asked "What happened to you in there?": **"That noise, it hurt me, when Hades touched
him. You didn't hear it? Feel anything?"** — answered **"Nothing. Nothing at all… I don't
know why."** — `[Riddy] What?` — and Prometheus: **"He will soon."** The E08 payoff
confirms the direction: `[Caeneus gasps]` at the Frame's ringing when his mother passes
through — that is when "he will soon" lands.

- [x] `01-CHARACTERS.md` › Caeneus › Arc: "physically feels the extraction, which Riddy
      does not" → reverse.
- [x] `02-COSMOLOGY.md` › §2 › Manual extraction: "Caeneus physically feels it… Riddy…
      feels nothing at all" → reverse; add the "He will soon" line and its E08 payoff.
- [x] `03-EPISODES.md` › E05 beat 15 ("Caeneus is knocked down by the pain; Riddy feels
      nothing") and E05 "What this plants" ("Caeneus's inexplicable sensitivity to
      soul-extraction") → reverse: Riddy's sensitivity, Caeneus's inexplicable *immunity*.
- [x] `05-MYSTERIES.md` › O6 › Evidence: "he physically feels Hades extracting Nax; Riddy…
      feels nothing" → reverse, with correction note.

Knock-on check: the corrected reading *helps* rather than hurts — Caeneus's immunity is the
anomaly that rhymes with Medusa's flat "Caeneus can get in the water" (E08), and Riddy's
sensitivity foreshadows her prophet arc. **D3 in `11-S2-CANON.md` does not depend on the
reversed claim** (verified — its mechanism is will + love + carrier, argued from E08).
- [x] When revising drafts, grep chapters 4/10/15/16 for any echo of "he felt Nax's
      extraction" — nothing in the bible relies on it, but a drafted sentence might.

### 1.2 Dionysus returned a *replacement* watch, not the original (E04)
The original went to the Fates at the Cave in E02 ("Ticktock… Take it. Take it."). In E04

one!") and gives *that* to Zeus ("Found this. By the gate"). Zeus wears a counterfeit for
the rest of the season — which is exactly why seeing the real one on Lachesis's wrist in
E08 detonates ("Wait… Where'd you get that watch?").

- [x] `03-EPISODES.md` › E04 beat 14: "then returns the original to Zeus" → "then passes
      the replacement off to Zeus as the original."
- [x] Optional: R7 in `05-MYSTERIES.md` is technically correct but could add the
      counterfeit detail — it sharpens the E08 reveal.

### 1.3 Prometheus's freeing is mistimed in `01-CHARACTERS.md`
